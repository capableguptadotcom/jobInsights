01

AI engineer ≈ product + backend + evaluation

RAG and agents appear with APIs, data systems, observability, reliability, and product ownership—not as prompt-only work.

02

ML engineer splits into model and platform tracks

One track builds domain models; the other owns serving, GPUs, inference, data pipelines, rollback, and on-call reliability.

03

Data scientist remains domain-heavy

Banking, retail, supply chain, healthcare, pricing, and analytics roles reward statistics and business framing alongside deployment.

04

“Visa support,” H-1B transfer, and new/unspecified H-1B sponsorship are different leads. The tracker never collapses them.

Interview signal

What candidates must be ready to prove

First-person stories are kept separate from job facts and marked by confidence.

1

Coding & core CS

Write clean executable code, reason about data structures, tests, failure cases, and interfaces.

2

ML fundamentals

Defend baselines, metrics, bias/variance, experiment design, error analysis, and model choice.

3

ML/LLM system design

Design data, retrieval, training, serving, evaluation, caching, monitoring, and rollback paths.

4

Production case study

Show latency, cost, reliability, quality, and user impact—not only architecture diagrams.

5

Domain & behavioral depth

Explain why the problem mattered, trade-offs made, stakeholders aligned, and outcomes measured.

2–3 year learning bet

Build a production-shaped profile.

For a candidate around six years, depth beats collecting more framework logos.

  1. FoundationPython + one backend ecosystem + SQL

    Own APIs, data contracts, testing, profiling, and software design.

  2. IntelligenceOne model/retrieval workflow with real evaluation

    Build baselines, error taxonomy, offline tests, and a human review loop.

  3. OperationsDeploy, observe, and recover

    CI/CD, containers, model/prompt versioning, latency, cost, monitoring, and rollback.

  4. DomainChoose a hard workflow

    Fintech risk, healthcare documents, search/ranking, speech, SRE, or supply chain.

  5. EvidencePublish two defensible case studies

    Show the decisions, metrics, failures, and measurable outcome—not a demo video alone.

Operating system

How the tracker stays honest

Evidence ladder

A: live company/ATS page. B: verified platform discovery. C: third-party or anonymous-client lead. Conclusions publish evidence coverage.

Deduplicate

Use company + requisition ID; fall back to normalized title + location + posted date. Cross-posts are not extra demand.

Track state

Active confirmed, stale unconfirmed, closed explicit, or closed inferred after two failed observations.

Reject scams

No fees, no personal-domain recruiter, no unrelated redirect, no unverifiable company, and no document sharing before role identity is established.
